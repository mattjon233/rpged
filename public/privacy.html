Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

RPGed respects user privacy.

We collect only the information necessary to operate the platform and improve the user experience.

This website

  • We set no cookies and show no ads.
  • Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, a measurement tool that works without cookies.
  • Your language choice is saved in your own browser so the page opens in the same language next time. It never leaves your device.
  • The site is delivered through Cloudflare, which processes technical data such as IP addresses to serve the pages and keep them secure.

When you write to us

If you email us, we receive your address and whatever you choose to include. We use that information only to answer you.
